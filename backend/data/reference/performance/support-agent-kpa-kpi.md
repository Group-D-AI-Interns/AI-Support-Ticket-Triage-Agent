# SUPPORT AGENT
# KEY PERFORMANCE AREAS (KPA) & KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPI)

**Department:** IT Service Management / Customer Support  
**Job Title:** Support Agent  
**Reports To:** Support Lead  
**Document Owner:** Human Resources / IT Operations  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. POSITION PURPOSE

The Support Agent is responsible for delivering professional, timely, and effective technical and customer support services to clients.

The role involves receiving, recording, investigating, resolving, and escalating incidents and service requests in accordance with organizational procedures, client classifications, priority rules, and Service Level Agreements (SLAs).

The Support Agent is accountable for ensuring that every client receives appropriate support according to their contractual service entitlement.

## 2. KEY PERFORMANCE AREAS

The Support Agent will be evaluated against the following eight Key Performance Areas.

### KPA 1: SLA Compliance and Ticket Management

**Weight: 20%**

Objective: Ensure that all assigned incidents and service requests are managed within the applicable SLA requirements.

Key Performance Indicators:

| KPI | Performance Target |
|---|---:|
| Tickets responded to within SLA | ≥ 95% |
| Tickets resolved within SLA | ≥ 90% |
| Tickets acknowledged within required timeframes | ≥ 95% |
| SLA breaches properly documented | 100% |

Expected Responsibilities:
- Monitor assigned tickets and SLA countdowns.
- Prioritize tickets according to approved priority rules.
- Apply the correct client classification.
- Escalate tickets before SLA deadlines are missed.
- Ensure that ticket statuses are updated.
- Follow up on outstanding incidents.

### KPA 2: First Contact Resolution and Technical Effectiveness

**Weight: 20%**

Objective: Resolve incidents efficiently and accurately without unnecessary escalations or repeated client interactions.

Key Performance Indicators:

| KPI | Performance Target |
|---|---:|
| First Contact Resolution (FCR) | ≥ 70% |
| First-Level Resolution Rate | ≥ 75% |
| Reopened Ticket Rate | ≤ 5% |
| Correct Technical Resolution | ≥ 95% |

Expected Responsibilities:
- Perform effective initial troubleshooting.
- Identify root causes where possible.
- Apply approved technical solutions.
- Avoid unnecessary escalations.
- Verify that the issue has been resolved.
- Document technical solutions for future reference.

### KPA 3: Client Experience and Customer Satisfaction

**Weight: 15%**

Objective: Deliver a professional and positive client experience throughout the support process.

Key Performance Indicators:

| KPI | Performance Target |
|---|---:|
| Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) | ≥ 90% |
| Professional Communication Compliance | ≥ 95% |
| Client Complaint Rate | ≤ 2% |
| Client Updates Provided as Required | ≥ 95% |

Expected Responsibilities:
- Communicate professionally with clients.
- Acknowledge client concerns.
- Provide accurate progress updates.
- Manage expectations regarding resolution times.
- Treat all client categories respectfully.
- Follow the applicable communication standards for Diamond, Platinum, Gold, Silver, and Bronze clients.

### KPA 4: Ticket Documentation and Data Accuracy

**Weight: 15%**

Objective: Maintain accurate, complete, and auditable records of all support activities.

Key Performance Indicators:

| KPI | Performance Target |
|---|---:|
| Ticket Documentation Compliance | ≥ 98% |
| Correct Priority Classification | ≥ 95% |
| Correct Client Classification | 100% |
| Complete Resolution Notes | ≥ 98% |

Expected Responsibilities:
- Record accurate incident descriptions.
- Capture troubleshooting steps.
- Document actions taken and outcomes.
- Record client communication.
- Maintain correct ticket categories and statuses.
- Ensure that closure notes are meaningful and complete.

### KPA 5: Productivity and Workload Management

**Weight: 10%**

Objective: Manage assigned workload efficiently while maintaining service quality.

Key Performance Indicators:

| KPI | Performance Target |
|---|---:|
| Assigned Ticket Backlog Management | Within agreed team thresholds |
| Tickets Updated Within Required Intervals | ≥ 95% |
| Work Queue Management Compliance | ≥ 95% |
| Scheduled Work and Shift Adherence | ≥ 95% |

Expected Responsibilities:
- Manage the assigned ticket queue.
- Monitor aging tickets.
- Avoid unnecessary ticket delays.
- Maintain appropriate availability during working hours.
- Complete assigned tasks within agreed timelines.
- Inform the Support Lead of workload constraints.

### KPA 6: Escalation and Incident Management

**Weight: 10%**

Objective: Ensure incidents requiring additional technical or management intervention are escalated correctly and timeously.

Key Performance Indicators:

| KPI | Performance Target |
|---|---:|
| Correct Escalation Rate | ≥ 95% |
| P1 Incident Escalation Compliance | 100% |
| Escalation Documentation Compliance | 100% |

Expected Responsibilities:
- Recognize incidents beyond personal technical authority.
- Escalate P1 and P2 incidents appropriately.
- Provide complete technical handover notes.
- Engage relevant technical teams.
- Follow up on escalated tickets.
- Participate in major incident communication when required.

### KPA 7: Knowledge Management and Continuous Improvement

**Weight: 5%**

Objective: Contribute to the organization's knowledge base and improve support processes.

Key Performance Indicators:

| KPI | Performance Target |
|---|---:|
| Knowledge Base Contributions | At least 1 per quarter |
| Knowledge Article Usage | As applicable |
| Identified Process Improvements | At least 1 per quarter |

Expected Responsibilities:
- Use approved knowledge articles.
- Share recurring solutions.
- Identify frequently occurring incidents.
- Recommend improvements.
- Participate in knowledge-sharing sessions.

### KPA 8: Professional Conduct and Compliance

**Weight: 5%**

Objective: Maintain professional conduct and comply with organizational policies and procedures.

Key Performance Indicators:

| KPI | Performance Target |
|---|---:|
| Policy and Procedure Compliance | 100% |
| Information Security Compliance | 100% |
| Attendance and Punctuality | ≥ 95% |
| Mandatory Training Completion | 100% |

Expected Responsibilities:
- Protect confidential client information.
- Follow information security requirements.
- Maintain professional workplace conduct.
- Comply with organizational policies.
- Attend mandatory training.
- Maintain punctuality and accountability.

## 5. PERFORMANCE ASSESSMENT METHOD

Performance must be assessed using measurable evidence from:

- Service management system reports.
- SLA performance dashboards.
- Customer satisfaction surveys.
- Ticket quality audits.
- Escalation records.
- Supervisor observations.
- Attendance and training records.

The Support Lead must conduct monthly performance monitoring and quarterly formal performance reviews.

## 6. PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT REQUIREMENTS

Where an employee consistently fails to achieve agreed performance targets:

1. The Support Lead must identify the performance gap.
2. The employee must receive documented feedback.
3. Appropriate coaching or training must be provided.
4. An improvement plan must be developed where necessary.
5. Progress must be reviewed during the following assessment period.

Performance assessments must consider ticket complexity, workload allocation, system availability, dependencies, and factors outside the employee's reasonable control.
