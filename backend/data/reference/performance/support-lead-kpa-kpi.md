# SUPPORT LEAD
# KEY PERFORMANCE AREAS (KPA) & KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPI)

**Department:** IT Service Management / Customer Support  
**Job Title:** Support Lead  
**Reports To:** IT Operations Manager / Service Delivery Manager  
**Document Owner:** Human Resources / IT Operations  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Review Frequency:** Monthly Performance Monitoring / Quarterly Formal Assessment  

---

## 1. POSITION PURPOSE

The Support Lead is responsible for managing, supervising, and coordinating the daily operations of the Support Team to ensure efficient service delivery, achievement of SLA commitments, operational effectiveness, and customer satisfaction.

The role provides leadership and direction to Support Agents while ensuring that incidents and service requests are managed according to organizational policies, priority rules, client classifications, and contractual service commitments.

The Support Lead is accountable for the overall performance, productivity, quality, and operational discipline of the support function.

## 2. KEY PERFORMANCE AREAS

The Support Lead will be evaluated against the following nine Key Performance Areas.

### KPA 1: Team SLA Performance and Service Delivery

**Weight: 20%**

Objective: Ensure that the Support Team consistently meets contractual service commitments and internal operational targets.

Key Performance Indicators:

| KPI | Performance Target |
|---|---:|
| Overall Team SLA Compliance | ≥ 95% |
| Diamond Client SLA Compliance | ≥ 98% |
| Platinum Client SLA Compliance | ≥ 97% |
| Gold Client SLA Compliance | ≥ 95% |
| Silver Client SLA Compliance | ≥ 93% |
| Bronze Client SLA Compliance | ≥ 90% |
| SLA Breach Investigation Compliance | 100% |

Expected Responsibilities:
- Monitor team SLA performance.
- Ensure correct application of client classifications.
- Identify tickets approaching SLA breaches.
- Ensure appropriate ticket allocation.
- Implement corrective action for recurring SLA failures.
- Escalate significant service delivery risks to management.
- Provide regular SLA performance reports.

- Address productivity concerns.

## 5. PERFORMANCE ASSESSMENT METHOD

The Support Lead's performance must be assessed using measurable operational evidence, including:

- Team SLA performance dashboards.
- Service management reports.
- Ticket quality audit results.
- Client satisfaction surveys.
- Major incident reports.
- Team productivity reports.
- Employee performance records.
- Coaching and training records.
- Management reports.
- Process improvement registers.
- Compliance and audit findings.

The IT Operations Manager or Service Delivery Manager must conduct monthly performance monitoring and quarterly formal performance reviews.

## 6. PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT REQUIREMENTS

Where the Support Lead fails to achieve agreed performance targets:

1. The responsible manager must identify the performance gap.
2. The Support Lead must receive documented feedback.
3. Root causes must be investigated.
4. Corrective action must be agreed.
5. Additional management support or development must be provided where necessary.
6. Progress must be assessed during subsequent review periods.

The Support Lead must demonstrate accountability for team performance while recognizing factors outside the team's control.

## 7. AUTHORITY AND ACCOUNTABILITY

The Support Lead is authorized to:

- Allocate and reassign support tickets.
- Monitor team workloads.
- Request technical escalations.
- Recommend priority reviews.
- Conduct quality audits.
- Recommend employee training.
- Initiate performance improvement discussions.
- Escalate resource constraints.
- Recommend operational process improvements.

The Support Lead may not independently change contractual client classifications, approve unauthorized SLA exceptions, or amend client service commitments.
